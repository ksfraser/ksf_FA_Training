## UC-FA-TR-001: Training Cost Tracking
**Actor**: Finance, HR

**FA-Specific Flow**:
1. Schedule training course
2. Assign employees
3. Track costs via FA GL:
   - Course fees
   - Employee time
4. Generate training expense report

---

## UC-FA-TR-002: Compliance Report
**Actor**: HR Manager, Compliance

**FA-Specific Flow**:
1. Generate compliance report in FA
2. Shows:
   - Required training by employee
   - Completion status
   - Expiration dates
3. Export for audit
